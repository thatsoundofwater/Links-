bl@nk Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

bl@nk (“the app”) is made by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics. When you use a tool, what you give it is sent to the services that do the work, and that is all.

1. What the app can access, and why

2. Who receives your data (including AI providers)

Many tools use artificial intelligence. When you use one, the photo, audio clip, text, or approximate location needed for that request is sent, through our secure backend, to the third-party service that processes it, and the result is sent back to you. Depending on the tool, these are:

ServiceWhat it receivesUsed for
OpenAIImages, audio, and text for the tool you are usingUnderstanding photos and audio, writing responses, generating images
ElevenLabsText to be spokenVoice narration
SupabaseThe same request, passing through our backendHosting the backend that talks to the providers above
Pl@ntNetPlant photosPlant identification
Open-MeteoApproximate coordinatesWeather forecasts
Other public lookups (movie, music, book, product, recipe, currency and word databases)The title, barcode, word, or search text you enteredFetching details you asked for

These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. They may keep requests for a limited time for security and abuse monitoring. Our backend does not store your photos or audio. Short-lived technical logs used to fix errors may briefly include text you typed. We do not sell your data and we do not allow providers to use what you send for advertising.

The app asks for your permission before your first AI request, and you can withdraw it at any time in the app’s settings.

3. What stays on your device

Saved items such as recordings, Cycle entries, favorites, drafts, history, and your preferences are stored on your device. We do not receive copies. Deleting the app deletes them.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins, or profiles.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Sharing

If you tap Share on a result, the app creates an image or file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Shared images carry a small bl@nk watermark.

6. Music previews, stems and copyright

Song ID uses the short preview clips provided by public music catalogs. bl@nk does not provide full songs. A tool can separate a 30-second preview into a vocal track and an instrumental track, and you can save or share those files. They are for your personal, creative use, such as learning, practice and sampling ideas. Do not post, sell, stream or release other people’s music, or audio made from it, without permission from the rights holder. You are responsible for how you use and share what you create. If you are a rights holder and want something addressed, contact us below.

7. Children

bl@nk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like related requests removed, contact us below.

8. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke camera, microphone, photos, or location access in iOS Settings; tools that need them will simply be unavailable.
  • Withdraw AI-processing permission in the app’s settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Because we do not keep an account for you, we generally hold no personal data to access or delete. If you think we do, contact us and we will look into it.

9. Security

Requests travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we keep our backend minimal and do not build a profile of you.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and if the change is significant we will tell you in the app.
