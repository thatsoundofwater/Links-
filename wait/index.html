w@it

support & privacy · Last updated: November 14, 2026

w@it (can't w@it) turns the things you're waiting for into loading bars on your lock screen and home screen. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Support

Questions, ideas, or something not working? Email ThatSoundOfWater@gmail.com.

Adding widgets

  • Lock screen: long-press the lock screen, tap Customize, tap the widget area, and choose w@it.
  • Home screen: long-press an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for w@it.
  • Pick which countdown a widget shows: after adding it, long-press the widget (or tap it while customizing the lock screen), tap the countdown option, and choose one.
  • If a widget looks out of date, remove it and add it again.

Privacy

Your countdowns and settings are stored only on your device, in space shared between the app and its widgets so they can show your countdowns. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Notifications

If you turn on "notify when done" for a countdown, the app asks permission to send notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification content is sent to a server.

Sharing

When you share a countdown, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts.

Children

w@it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
