fl@me

support & privacy · Last updated: November 15, 2026

fl@me is a concert lighter that feels the music. The flame reacts to live sound or to songs you choose, and you can record short clips to share. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

Support

Questions, ideas, or something not working? Email ThatSoundOfWater@gmail.com.

How it works

  • live: the flame listens to the room through the microphone and reacts to the music around you. Blow on the phone to put it out.
  • music: pick songs from your Files and the flame burns to the beat.
  • camera: film the show with the flame reacting underneath. Clips are 30 seconds at most.
  • your room: pick one of your own photos or Live Photos to flash behind the flame.
  • Tap fl@me at the top of the app for more tips.

Privacy

Everything fl@me does happens on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Microphone

When you turn on live or record a clip, the app uses the microphone to measure the sound around you so the flame can react, and to put that sound in your clip. The sound is analyzed on your device as it plays and is not stored or uploaded. A clip is saved only when you record one, and only on your device.

Camera

The camera is used only in camera mode, to show and record the show you're filming. Video stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Photos and music

Photos you pick for "your room" are copied into the app on your device so they can be shown again later; deleting the app removes them. Songs you pick are played from your device. The app asks to add to your photo library only so you can save a clip there.

Sharing

When you finish a clip, the app hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts.

Children

fl@me does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
