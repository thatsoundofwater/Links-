ch@nt privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

ch@nt is a meditation and affirmation app. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. This page explains the small amount of information the app handles.

What stays on your device

The phrases you type, your recent and pinned phrases, your streak, and your settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them as an account or profile. Deleting the app removes them.

Voice generation

To speak your phrase in a natural voice, the app sends the text of the phrase, along with the voice and tone you chose, to our server, which passes it to ElevenLabs to generate the audio. The audio is returned to your device and saved there so the same phrase does not need to be generated again. We do not keep a record of your phrases on our server. ElevenLabs processes the text under its own privacy policy. If the service is unavailable, the app falls back to a voice built into your phone, and nothing is sent.

Abuse prevention

To stop automated abuse of the voice service, our server temporarily counts requests using a random identifier generated by the app on your device (not linked to your name, Apple ID, or contacts) and your network address. These counters are used only to apply usage limits and are deleted after a short time.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone to dictate a phrase, the app uses Apple's speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Audio is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and is not stored or received by us. The microphone is used only while you are dictating.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. We only learn whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock premium sounds.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts.

Health disclaimer

ch@nt is for relaxation and general wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Descriptions of frequencies reflect traditional associations, not medical claims. Talk to a healthcare professional about any health concern.

Children

ch@nt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
